## 1. cataract-surgery — reviewers: both doctors

### Medical statements to check
1. Cataract = clouding of the natural lens; cannot be removed by drops or medicines.
2. Symptoms list: blurred/hazy vision, glare and halos, faded colours, frequent spectacle change, monocular double vision.
3. Surgery is timed by impact on daily life, not by cataract presence alone; earlier surgery may be advised for very dense cataract or when it blocks management of another eye condition.

7. Risks listed: infection/inflammation, pressure rise, rare retinal complications; posterior capsule opacification treated with a later laser.
8. IOL choice described only as distance / near / both — no brand or price.
9. Urgent-help criteria: increasing pain, redness, discharge, sudden vision drop after surgery.
