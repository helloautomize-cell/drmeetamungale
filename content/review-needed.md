# Glossary — pending doctor approval

Every term below is shown to patients with `needsReview: true` in
`content/glossary.ts`. Please approve or correct the plain-language wording.

| Term | Full name | Draft explanation |
|---|---|---|
| SLT | Selective Laser Trabeculoplasty | A gentle laser treatment applied to the eye's drainage tissue to help fluid leave the eye more easily and lower eye pressure. |
| Nd:YAG Laser Peripheral Iridotomy | — | A tiny laser opening made at the edge of the iris, the coloured part of the eye, to improve fluid flow in narrow-angle eyes. |
| Conventional glaucoma surgery | trabeculectomy and drainage implants | Surgery that creates a new route for fluid to leave the eye, or places a small drainage tube, to lower eye pressure when drops and lasers are not enough. |
| MIGS | Minimally Invasive Glaucoma Surgery | Small-incision procedures that improve the eye's own drainage with less tissue disturbance. Suitable for selected patients. |
| GATT | Gonioscopy-Assisted Transluminal Trabeculotomy | A microsurgical procedure that opens the eye's natural drainage channel from the inside through a very small incision. |
| Trabeculotomy | for congenital glaucoma | Surgery that opens the drainage channel in infants and children born with an abnormally developed drainage system. |
| AGV | Ahmed Glaucoma Valve (drainage implant) | A small drainage device placed in the eye to carry fluid out and lower pressure. |
| MGD | Meibomian Gland Dysfunction | Blockage of the tiny oil glands in the eyelids, a common cause of dry eye. |
| IDRA | Dry Eye Analysis (IDRA) | _empty — TODO(doctor): needs the doctor's own wording_ |
| E-Eye | E-Eye | _empty — TODO(doctor): needs the doctor's own wording_ |
| DALK | Deep Anterior Lamellar Keratoplasty | Replaces the front and middle layers of a diseased cornea while keeping the patient's own inner layer. |
| DSEK | Descemet's Stripping Endothelial Keratoplasty | Replaces only the inner layer of the cornea when its inner cells have failed. |
| DMEK | Descemet Membrane Endothelial Keratoplasty | Replaces the thinnest inner layer of the cornea with donor tissue. |
| Keratoplasty | corneal transplantation | Corneal transplantation, replacing diseased or scarred cornea with healthy donor tissue. |
| CXL | Corneal Collagen Cross-linking | Strengthens a thinning, weak cornea (keratoconus) using riboflavin drops and ultraviolet light to slow progression. |
| LASIK | Laser-Assisted In Situ Keratomileusis | Laser reshaping of the cornea to correct short-sightedness, long-sightedness and astigmatism. |
| PRK | Photorefractive Keratectomy | Surface laser vision correction, suited to some patients with thinner corneas. |
| PTK | Phototherapeutic Keratectomy | Laser treatment that smooths or removes surface corneal irregularities and scars. |
| SMILE | Small Incision Lenticule Extraction | Keyhole laser vision correction without a flap. |
| SLET | Simple Limbal Epithelial Transplantation | Transfers a small amount of healthy surface cells to help repair a damaged corneal surface. |
| Phacoemulsification | — | Cataract surgery in which ultrasound gently breaks up the cloudy lens through a small incision, followed by placing an artificial lens. |
| SICS | Small Incision Cataract Surgery | Cataract removal through a small self-sealing incision, often used for very dense cataracts. |
| ECCE | Extracapsular Cataract Extraction | Traditional cataract surgery, removing the lens in one piece through a larger incision. |
| IOL | Intraocular Lens | The artificial lens placed inside the eye to replace the clouded natural lens. |
| OCT | Optical Coherence Tomography | A painless scan that takes cross-section images of the retina, optic nerve or cornea. |
| IOP | Intraocular Pressure | The fluid pressure inside the eye. |
| Specular microscopy | — | A scan that photographs and counts the cells lining the inside of the cornea. |
| Topography | — | A scan that maps the curve of the front surface of the cornea. |
